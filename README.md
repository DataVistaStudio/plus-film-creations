# Plus Film Creations

**Public project reference** · [plusfilmcreations.org](https://plusfilmcreations.org/)

Plus Film Creations is a Lebanon-based creative initiative exploring cinema, community stories, and learning through filmmaking.

### Projects

- **SLD Film Festival** — a project dedicated to the stories and cultural memory of South Lebanon.
- **Home of Kid Filmmakers** — activities and opportunities for children to explore filmmaking.

The [official website](https://plusfilmcreations.org/) provides Arabic, English and French information. Project dates, film submission periods, venues, festival eligibility and FilmFreeway links are official **only after they appear on the website**.
